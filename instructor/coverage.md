# Proposed capabilities and practice coverage

This map covers the draft's proposed learning capabilities, not an approved
syllabus or an examination specification. Each linked exercise has a hint and
worked solution. Notes state the model conditions and reading focus.

| Proposed capability | Main teaching units | Representative evidence of practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Track amounts, loads, and compartments | Environmental systems; deposition; integrated case | [Mixing and load exercises](../environmental-systems/exercise.qmd); [annual catchment budget](../integrated-case/exercise.qmd) |
| 2. Explain transformations using reactions, equilibrium, and rates | Atmospheric reactions; aquatic equilibrium; redox; waste | [Catalytic cancellation and lifetime](../atmospheric-reactions/exercise.qmd); [electron/atom/charge bookkeeping](../redox-cycles/exercise.qmd); [pyrite endpoint](../waste-leachate/exercise.qmd) |
| 3. Calculate simple speciation, partitioning, and saturation | Aquatic equilibrium; carbonate/gases; metals | [Weak-acid and ionic-strength cases](../aquatic-equilibria/exercise.qmd); [carbonate, Henry, and calcite problems](../carbonate-gases/exercise.qmd); [ligand and hydroxide balances](../metals/exercise.qmd) |
| 4. Explain effects of organic matter, redox, and surfaces | Organic matter; surfaces; redox; soil | [Oxygen and binding](../aquatic-organic-matter/exercise.qmd); [two-phase sorption](../surfaces/exercise.qmd); [exchange inventories](../soil-chemistry/exercise.qmd) |
| 5. Relate organic structure to distribution and persistence | Organic contaminants; climate chemistry for a separate gas/radiation context | [Ionisation, Koc, and parent half-life](../organic-contaminants/exercise.qmd); [why spectral and lifetime properties differ](../climate-chemistry/exercise.qmd) |
| 6. Interpret data/model plots and communicate reproducibly | Computing activities A/B/C; treatment; integrated case | [Water-table report](../aquatic-equilibria/exercise.qmd#activity-a-water-table-in-r); [speciation report](../carbonate-gases/exercise.qmd); [fate report and synthesis](../organic-contaminants/exercise.qmd#activity-c-a-fate-model-in-r) |

The treatment unit transfers the same chemistry into a process context:
[sequential removal, coagulant acidity, and nitrogen form](../water-treatment/exercise.qmd).
The final case requires a balanced reaction, numerical support, a stated
limitation, and a measurement that could distinguish alternatives. The draft
introduces no additional statistical methods and requires no specialist
speciation package or student-written equilibrium solver.
